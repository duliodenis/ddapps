Questions, answered

Codes are read aloud. Trust is confirmed. The pairing stays in your pocket.

What is a secret?+

A secret is a private phrase that generates your HumanCodes. You share it with exactly one person, and it stays on your phones.

What happens after 60 seconds?+

The current codes change and both phones independently generate the next matching pair.

Can I unpair someone?+

Yes. Delete any pairing from your friends list anytime.
