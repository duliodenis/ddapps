Jix + Jox

Two ways to find the word

Switch between both daily games without leaving Today. Each keeps its own time, progress, and streak.

Jix

Put every letter in its place

Unscramble four rounds, read the tile feedback, and bank the letters that lead to the Final.

  1. 1Move letters into the answer row
  2. 2Use locked and outlined tiles as feedback
  3. 3Solve the Final and complete the day
Jox

Trace every target through the grid

Drag across adjacent letters to find the listed words. Paths can bend, diagonals count, and each cell can be used only once per word.

  1. 1Start on any letter in a target word
  2. 2Trace through eight-direction adjacency
  3. 3Find every target and beat your best time
Jix + Jox clues

A little light when you need it

Both games offer a focused clue when you get stuck, without giving away the answer.

Jix: check your positionsAfter 30 seconds, correctly positioned letters glow yellow. Try again every 10 seconds while the row remains unsolved.
Jox: find your starting pointTap the lightbulb to highlight the first cell of a remaining target, then trace the path yourself.
